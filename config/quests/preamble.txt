Year 0. Your fusion startup has funding for twenty years and zero working reactors.
The investors want a power plant. The universe wants you to fail. Your cofounder wants a foosball table.

You do not control the grind. The grind controls you. Fight mobs, collect junk, sell junk, level up.
Main quests move the company forward. Side hustles distract you. You will chase side hustles. Everyone does.

Twenty years from now the clock hits zero. Be ready, or at least look ready.
